Tuition fees are set on an annual basis and vary by year group, covering costs associated with academic tuition, the use of School facilities, most co-curricular activities, and student support services.
Please note that fees are reviewed annually and may be subject to change.

Fee Policy and Related Conditions of Enrolment
1. Application Fees are payable at the time of lodgement of the Application for Enrolment. The Application Fee is non-refundable.
2. Enrolment and Confirmation Fees are payable within one month of request by the School. These fees are non-refundable. If these Fees are not paid by the due date, the agreement formed by the acceptance of the School’s offer of a position for the student automatically terminates.
3. All fees for tuition, resource levy, technology levy, compulsory and optional camps, boarding, extra subjects, excursions and the supply of goods and services to the student are determined by the School Council from time to time and will be notified to parents/guardians. All fees are due and payable by the first day of term.

4. If fees and/or charges are not paid by the due date, a monthly charge (“Overdue Charge”) of $250 will be charged until the date of payment.
5. If fees and/or charges are not paid in full by the end of the term in which they were due for payment, the student’s enrolment may be suspended and the School may subsequently without further notice refuse entry to the student or terminate his/her enrolment.
6. A full term’s notice in writing must be given to the Headmaster before any student is removed from the School. If this notice is not given, a term’s fees and charges plus GST are payable.
7. No remission of fees, either in whole or in part, will be made if a student is absent for any reason.
8. If the School incurs expenditure on behalf of parents/guardians such as purchases of books, stationery and equipment, or advances to the student such fares from time to time as the School considers necessary, the amounts incurred or advanced must be paid on request.
9. All medical and ambulance expenses incurred on behalf of the student are payable by the parent/guardian.
10. Should a student be removed from the School by a parent/guardian for an extended amount of time and the parent/guardian wishes to hold a position for the student’s return, a “Holding Fee”, currently equivalent to five weeks’ full fees and one quarter of the annual fees for the time the student will not be at Shore is payable. This fee is payable in one lump sum at the time of leaving the school and guarantees re-entry Day 1, Term 1 of the agreed re-entry year. Should a student require earlier return than the agreed date, there is no guarantee that such entry would be possible. If a student returns during an academic year, full fees are payable for the whole of that academic year. Parents/guardians should contact the Registrar to discuss requirements for holding a place for a student.
11. The Federal Government has enacted legislation to cover students entering from overseas who do not travel on Australian passports or have not been granted entry through migration. Shore is approved to conduct secondary courses (CRICOS Provider Code 00772M). As the Government subsidy is not payable for such students, the School is required to levy an additional amount to cover the subsidy foregone. This levy is a 20% surcharge on the tuition fees paid by domestic students.
12. A discount of 25% on boarding fees will apply to the second son, where boys are boarding concurrently, provided the sons have the same mother and father. The discount will be automatically applied to the younger sibling’s invoice while both siblings remain enrolled at Shore. Please note that if the elder sibling leaves the School, the discount will no longer apply from that point forward. The sibling discount cannot be added to or combined with any existing or other scholarships or bursaries. If the second child is in receipt of a bursary or scholarship, the larger discount will apply. 
13. A discount of 50% on boarding fees will apply to third and fourth sons. 100% discount on boarding fees will apply to the fifth son provided all the sons have the same mother and father. The discount applies when all students in the family have boarded.
14. In addition to the compulsory camps there are a number of optional camps that will be charged separately.
